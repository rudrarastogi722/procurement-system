## 📌 1. Problem Statement
Construction projects require diverse materials at different construction stages. Delays and budget overruns frequently occur when procurement teams cannot easily track material requirements, compare supplier quotes, or monitor delivery statuses across different project sites.

## 🎯 2. Project Objective
Develop a centralized, web-based procurement management workflow system that organizes construction material requests, supplier communications, quotation comparisons, purchase orders, delivery tracking, and automated cost estimation.

## 🚀 3. Core Functional Modules
1. **Procurement Dashboard:** Real-time visibility into total requests, approved orders, pending shipments, material spend charts, and order status distribution.
2. **Material Requests Management:** Interactive form to raise, log, and filter material requisitions with site location, quantity, units, and urgency levels.
3. **Supplier Directory:** Central registry for tracking approved construction vendors, rating scores, contact details, and material specializations.
4. **Quotation Comparison:** Side-by-side evaluation of vendor bids with pricing, credit terms, and delivery lead times.
5. **Purchase Order (PO) Tracking:** Lifecycle tracking of generated POs with budget utilization gauges.
6. **Real-time Delivery Tracking:** Visual multi-stage shipment timeline for transit visibility.
7. **Cost Simulator:** Dynamic interactive calculator calculating material unit costs, custom quantities, GST percentages, transport, and labor expenses with real-time grand total generation.

## 💻 4. Technologies Used
- **HTML5:** Semantic document structure, forms, data tables, and input validations.
